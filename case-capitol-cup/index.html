The Problem

We were texting each other scores

The Capitol Cup is a one-day team match, 26 players a side, 27 holes that mix best ball, alternate shot, and singles into one running team total worth 65 points. Golf Genius, the software everyone uses, could not score our format live. So every year the same thing happened: to know if we were winning, captains texted each other match updates and did the math in their heads.

The tool everyone uses did not fit our event. That is the usual reason a real need goes unmet: the off-the-shelf thing is close, but it is not yours.

What I Built

One link the whole event runs on

The ask, in plain terms

One link my event runs on: a live leaderboard anyone can watch, a dead-simple way for each group to tap their score hole by hole, and it has to understand our format, best ball, alternate shot, and singles, all rolling into one team total.

That is what it built. Each group opens their own match link on a phone and taps A wins, halve, or B wins for the hole they just finished. Anyone else opens the leaderboard and watches the score move.

Nobody texts a score anymore. Scorekeepers tap. The team total updates on every phone in about five seconds.

How It Grew

Thirty minutes got the core. Then we kept leveling it up.

The first version worked after one thirty-minute session: tap a score, watch the leaderboard. Everything after that was focusing on what the guys actually needed and automating wherever we could.

Printed scorecards, dotted automatically

A print-ready card for each group. It calculates every player's course handicap from their index, then dots the card, marking exactly which holes each side gets a stroke. The math that usually starts the arguments, done for you.

Individual match view

Pull up any single group's live scorecard and follow one match without the whole board.

Starting holes

A shotgun start puts every group on a different hole. The app follows each group's real hole order.

The pairings party

A board projected on the TV to set the matchups the night before, more fun and more banter with the guys.

Thirty minutes got the core working. The printing, the match views, the pairings party, all came from watching the guys use it and removing every bit of friction.

Why It Matters

This was never about golf

Golf Genius is fine for a normal tournament. Ours was not normal, and when the standard tool is 80% right, most people just live with the missing 20% forever. This is the alternative: build the exact thing you need, for about the cost of a domain. Swap the golf event for your reporting, your intake, your ops board, the workflow no vendor sells because it is specific to you.
